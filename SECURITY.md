| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| 1.x | Yes |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately instead:

- through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/stickysoftware/bump-laravel/security/advisories/new) on this repository, or
- by email to [hello@bumpbunny.dev](mailto:hello@bumpbunny.dev).

Include what you found, the steps or code to reproduce it, the package, PHP and Laravel versions, and what someone could do with it. A proof of concept helps, but isn't required.

Every report gets an answer.
